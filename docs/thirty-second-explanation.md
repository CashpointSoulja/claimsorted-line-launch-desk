# 30-second explanation

> When a claims TPA takes on a new line of business, the client's handling spec arrives as a spreadsheet, and the risk is that gaps only show up after real claims are being handled. Line Launch Desk checks that spec against one shared claims framework before go-live. It rejects broken rows, deduplicates by stable rule ID, and holds launch while any stage lacks a signed-off, in-date rule within limits. Publishing needs two approvals bound to the exact pack hash, and it is idempotent and verified on re-read. After launch, it reads pilot metrics denominator-first and refuses to call a win when the blended number hides a case-mix shift. Whatever it finds becomes a tested product agenda instead of anecdotes. It's an independent concept with synthetic data only.

About 30 seconds read aloud (around 110 words).
