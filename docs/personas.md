# Hypothesis personas

These are **hypotheses, not research.** No interviews have been run. Names are invented. Each persona lists the assumption that would most change the design if it were wrong.

## 1. Priya, ClaimSorted ops lead (launch owner)
- **Goal:** take a new client line live on the agreed date with no handling gaps.
- **Today (assumed):** reconciles the client spreadsheet against internal procedures by hand and chases sign-offs over email.
- **Pain (assumed):** finds gaps (no complaint route, stale wording) after live claims arrive.
- **Uses the desk for:** the gate, the gap list, her approval, and publishing.
- **Riskiest assumption:** that launches are slowed by spec gaps rather than by tech integration work.

## 2. Tom, claims handler
- **Goal:** handle each claim correctly without checking three documents.
- **Today (assumed):** works from a playbook that may lag the client's latest rules.
- **Pain (assumed):** authority limits and SLAs that differ by client, and steps that don't fit the claim in front of him.
- **Uses the desk for:** indirectly, through the published pack. His skipped steps show up in adoption metrics.
- **Riskiest assumption:** that low adoption means the step is wrong for the work, not that he lacks training. The desk treats this as TO TEST.

## 3. Sam, product manager
- **Goal:** prioritise the roadmap on frontline evidence.
- **Today (assumed):** hears about problems through Slack and anecdotes.
- **Pain (assumed):** can't tell a systemic gap from a one-off.
- **Uses the desk for:** the product agenda, regression cases, and segmented metrics.
- **Riskiest assumption:** that ops-derived agenda items are specific enough to build from.

## 4. Grace, MGA client ops contact (Harbour Pet, fictional)
- **Goal:** get her line live with her rules applied exactly.
- **Today (assumed):** sends a spreadsheet and receives questions back in batches.
- **Pain (assumed):** repeated back-and-forth, and doubt about which version was approved.
- **Uses the desk for:** the gap list (as an export) and her programme-owner approval.
- **Riskiest assumption:** that clients will sign off inside a TPA's tool rather than on a PDF.
